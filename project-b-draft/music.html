Nauryz - a holiday of spring and renewal that is being celebrated by the Turkic peoples around the world. In Kazakhstan, Nauryz, which means "a new day", is being treated as a New Year, since its coming signifies a beginning of a new cycle after harsh winter. Considering that many Turkic people were nomads in the past, they had to move around according to weather conditions that vary throughout seasons; therefore, Nauryz always brings lots of joy and happiness as everything starts to blossom and the weather gets better. To support this spirit, Kazakh traditional instruments play a crucial role. Dombra and Kobyz are main instruments to create mood during the event!
