Kazakh traditional instruments play a crucial role in celebrating Nauryz. In ancient times, musical instruments were widely used in various ways during the nomadic life, such as during magical rites, hunting sessions, military maneuvers, and for kids and youth’s leisure activities. Kobyz is a bow instrument that consists of two strings made of horsehair. It is believed to be the most ancient bow instrument of the world. It includes three parts (head, middle, and lower part) and it is hollowed out of one single piece of wood. Dombra is the most popular Kazakh musical instrument different in shape, frets and fingerboard length. It also has only two strings and the music pieces played with it are called Kyui instruments to create mood during the event!
