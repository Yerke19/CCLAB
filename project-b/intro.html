Nauryz Meiramy

~ Kazakh New Year ~

Discover more!

Nauryz is a holiday of spring and renewal that is celebrated on March 21st-23rd by the Turkic peoples around the world. In Kazakhstan, Nauryz, which means "a new day", is being treated as a New Year, since its coming signifies a beginning of a new cycle after harsh winter. As many Turkic people were nomads in the past, they had to move around according to weather conditions that vary throughout seasons; therefore, Nauryz always brings lots of joy and happiness as everything starts to blossom and the weather gets better.
