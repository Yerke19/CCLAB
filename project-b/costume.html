Kazakh Traditional Costumes

Go Back
During Nauryz, big concerts are being held at the city squares, where many people dress up in traditional costumes. One of the main elements in Kazakh national clothes for women is “Kamzol”, which is a traditional jacket that can be put on dress and “Saukele”, which is a headwear that initially serves as a wedding hat. Men have costumes that consist of headwear, pants and “Shapan”, which is an outerwear sewed from velvet.
